# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Potential clients, businesses, event organizers, and property managers looking for professional security services in the Philippines (Manila/Legazpi).

## Product Purpose
A corporate website for MVPManila Security Agency to showcase their services, highlight their industry compliance (DOLE, PNP-SOSIA), display awards/certificates to build trust, and provide contact information.

## Brand Personality
Professional, secure, reliable, authoritative, and trustworthy.

## Anti-references
- Playful or overly casual designs
- Cluttered, overwhelming dashboard styles
- Sketchy or amateur illustrations
- Overly tech/SaaS "startup" templates with glowing gradients

## Design Principles
- **Authority through restraint:** Use space and structure to project confidence.
- **Clear visual hierarchy:** Make services and compliance easy to scan.
- **Trust via evidence:** Awards and client logos should feel earned and proudly displayed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Clear contrast for readability (WCAG AA).
- Accessible navigation for all users.
